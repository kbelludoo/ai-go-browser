## AI Go Browser Dual-Licensing Architecture

AI Go Browser is distributed under a **Dual-Licensing Model** designed to foster academic and open-source innovation while protecting commercial intellectual property and enterprise value.

---

### 1. Community Edition (Free & Open Source)
* **License:** [MIT License](LICENSE)
* **Target Audience:** Individual developers, academic researchers, student projects, and open-source hobbyists.
* **Permitted Use:** Modification, experimentation, benchmark reproduction, and community tool building.

---

### 2. Enterprise Commercial License (High-Scale & Commercial SaaS)
For commercial entities, AI labs, search engines, and multi-tenant SaaS platforms (such as Perplexity, OpenAI, Anthropic, Firecrawl, or enterprise scraping operators):

A **Commercial Enterprise Agreement** is required when:
1. **High Volume Deployment:** Running clusters processing over 100,000 web navigation tasks per month.
2. **Proprietary Embedding:** Integrating the engine into closed-source, fee-charging commercial AI platforms, APIs, or SaaS products without attribution or upstream requirements.
3. **Enterprise Compliance & Legal Indemnity:** Requiring corporate warranties of non-infringement, compliance assurances, and enterprise indemnification.
4. **Custom Integrations:** Needing C-ABI shared libraries (`libaigobrowser.so` / `aigobrowser.dll`), private Rust FFI bindings, or custom distributed worker orchestrators.
